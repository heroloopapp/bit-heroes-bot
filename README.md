## What the Bit Heroes bot does

- **Nine activities, back to back:** Quest, PvP, GvG, World Boss, Raid, Expedition, Invasion, Trials and Gauntlet. Tick the ones you want.
- **Fishing included:** casts at the maximum of 40, hooks on the bite, catches on the green 100% block and fishes until your bait runs out. More: [Bit Heroes fishing bot](https://heroloop.org/bit-heroes-fishing-bot.html)
- **Switches heroes by itself:** when one hero is out of energy, the next one plays. Nothing to set up.
- **Never spends gems:** every "purchase more?" prompt gets a No.
- **Screen and mouse only:** it reads the screen and clicks like a player. No memory editing, no injection, no packet tampering.
- **Steam and Kongregate:** the same app and key work with both.
- **One-click updates** while you are subscribed.

## Requirements

- Windows 10 or 11
- Bit Heroes in English, on Steam or on Kongregate in Chrome
- The game visible on your main monitor while it runs (Steam has an experimental background mode)

## How to start

1. Get HeroLoop at https://heroloop.org. Your license key arrives by email.
2. Run `HeroLoop.exe` (one file, nothing to install) and paste your key.
3. Pick a quest, tick activities, open the game and press **F9**. **F10** stops it.

Full guide: https://heroloop.org/guide.html
